# 21 — Provisional Domain Entity Catalogue

> **Note:** All entities below are **provisional** candidates for the external architect to evaluate. Existing Prisma models (`User`, `Card`, `Scan`, `PointsLedger`, `FaceOff`, etc.) are documented in [12-existing-database-schema.md](./12-existing-database-schema.md).

| Candidate Entity | Business Purpose | Repository Evidence | Key Properties | Relationships | Sensitivity & Lifecycle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`AtelierProduct`** | Physical skincare product in Atelier | `SkincareProductSpec` (`types.ts:9-19`), `skincare/page.tsx` | `id`, `slug`, `name`, `subtitle`, `category`, `ritualStep`, `description`, `suitability`, `active` | Has many `ProductVariant`, `ProductIngredient`, `RoutineStep` | Public catalog; soft-delete (`active`) |
| **`ProductVariant`** | Size/volume/SKU with authoritative price & stock | `volumeLabel`, `priceFormatted` (`types.ts:14-15`) | `id`, `productId`, `sku`, `volumeLabel`, `priceMinor` (Int), `currency`, `stockOnHand`, `stockReserved` | Belongs to `AtelierProduct`; referenced by `BagItem`, `OrderItem` | High consistency (stock locking/atomic decrement) |
| **`Ingredient`** | Botanical/active notes & incompatibilities | `keyNotes` (`types.ts:16`), `ProductDetailLayout.tsx:62-73` | `id`, `name`, `inciName`, `benefits`, `contraindications` | M:N with `AtelierProduct` | Public reference data |
| **`SkinProfile`** | User's voluntary skin goals, type & sensitivities | `selectedGoals` (`skincare/page.tsx:63`), `RoutineAndOrdersLayout.tsx` | `userId` (PK/FK), `skinType`, `goals[]`, `sensitivities[]`, `consentAt`, `updatedAt` | 1:1 `User` | **Sensitive health-adjacent PII** (NDPA); delete on account deletion |
| **`SkincareRoutine`** & **`RoutineStep`** | Personalized AM/PM regimen | `RoutineStepSlot` (`types.ts:34-40`), `PersonalRoutineLayout` | `id`, `userId`, `timeOfDay` (`AM`/`PM`), `stepNumber`, `stepTitle`, `productId?`, `isCustom` | Belongs to `User`; optional FK `AtelierProduct` | User-owned; deleted on account deletion |
| **`ShoppingBag`** & **`BagItem`** | Persistent cart before checkout | `BagLineItem` (`types.ts:21-24`), `ShoppingBagLayout` | `id`, `userId?`, `guestToken?`, `variantId`, `quantity`, `expiresAt` | Belongs to `User?`, `ProductVariant` | Ephemeral/TTL; converts to `Order` |
| **`Order`** & **`OrderItem`** | Immutable purchase record for Atelier | `OrderRecordPlaceholder` (`types.ts:26-32`), `CheckoutLayout` | `id`, `orderNumber`, `userId?`, `status`, `subtotalMinor`, `shippingMinor`, `taxMinor`, `totalMinor`, `currency`, `shippingAddress` (encrypted/JSON), `paymentRef` | Belongs to `User?`; has many `OrderItem`, `PaymentTransaction` | **Financial & PII**; immutable audit trail (anonymize PII on NDPA delete) |
| **`PaymentTransaction`** | Paystack payment & webhook state | `src/lib/paystack.ts` | `id`, `reference @unique`, `provider` (`PAYSTACK`), `amountMinor`, `currency`, `status`, `rawWebhookPayload`, `idempotencyKey` | Belongs to `Order?` or `User` | **Financial**; strict idempotency & signature verification |
| **`Friendship`** | Replaces `AuditLog` (`ADD_FRIEND`) hack | `src/app/api/friends/route.ts:35-42` | `id`, `requesterId`, `addresseeId`, `status` (`PENDING`/`ACCEPTED`/`BLOCKED`), `createdAt` | M:N self-relation on `User` | User-owned |
| **`Referral`** | Tracks invite codes & `+100 PTS` rewards | `src/app/friends/add/page.tsx:110,119` | `id`, `referrerId`, `referredUserId`, `code`, `rewardedAt` | `User` -> `User` | Anti-farming / idempotent reward |
